# Symnaps V2 product contract

The graph remains the landing experience and primary research workspace. Preserve brand, canvas interactions, authentication, analysis, clustering, imports and marketplace.

First slice: a versioned Thesis Card in the existing drawer. Store original captured hypothesis, current hypothesis, rationale, assets, sector, horizon, evaluation deadline, optional benchmark, measurable success condition, invalidation conditions and research workflow state. Offer Overview, Evidence, What Changed?, History and Decisions. Overview retains existing actions; History lists trusted snapshots; What Changed compares recorded revisions only. Evidence and Decisions explain their unavailable status without synthetic records.

Original means first trusted snapshot. Existing theses only have a migration baseline; their actual original text cannot be recovered reliably from editable legacy history. New revisions require a reason; concurrent edits must not overwrite one another. Server/database timestamps establish when information entered the system.

Financial language: conviction is a user/model belief; signal alignment is the fraction of fired predicates supporting a thesis; verified predictive accuracy requires predeclared measurable conditions and independently verified completed outcomes; realized performance requires actual decision/instrument/cashflow observations. Current legacy `accuracy` is ONLY signal alignment. Signal-confirmed is not a verified prediction. No performance or evidence is invented.

Private by default. Publishing is explicit. Do not expose private research history through public author metrics. AI relationship classification remains advisory; no new model provider dependency.
